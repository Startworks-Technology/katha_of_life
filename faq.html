FAQ

Questions parents ask

Everything you need to know about the classes. Can't find your answer? Ask Dr. Monica on WhatsApp.

Who can join the classes?

Pregnant mothers, their partners and new parents. Each course page shows which stage of pregnancy it is best for.

Can my husband or family attend with me?

Yes. The price is per registered email, so you can attend alone, as a couple or with your family at the same price.

Which languages are the classes taught in?

English and Telugu. You choose your language when you book.

Are the classes online or in person?

Right now all classes are live online, so you can join from home from anywhere in India. In-person classes in Vizag will start soon.

How do I book a seat?

Open a course and tap "Book in English" or "Book in Telugu". WhatsApp opens with your message ready, and our team will reply with the batch dates and payment details.

Are the prices inclusive of GST?

Yes. All course prices on this website are inclusive of GST.

When should I join the Labour Management course?

From the 5th month of pregnancy onwards. It is also helpful for second-time mothers.

When should I join the Breastfeeding course?

In the 3rd trimester, before your baby arrives, or as a new mother.

What are Dr. Monica's qualifications?

Dr. Monica is an internationally certified Childbirth Educator (CCCE, CAPPA) and a certified Advanced Lactation Professional (ACLP) from Begin Ed.

Do these classes replace my doctor's advice?

No. The classes are educational and work alongside your doctor. For anything urgent, contact your doctor or hospital straight away.
