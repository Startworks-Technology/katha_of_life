About Dr. Monica

Every birth is a story. I'm here to help you write yours.

"Katha" means story. I started Katha of Life because every life holds a story, and no parent should walk into birth or the first weeks of feeding feeling unprepared or alone.

As a Childbirth Educator and Advanced Certified Lactation Professional, I guide families through pregnancy, labour management and breastfeeding with warm, practical and evidence-based support.
